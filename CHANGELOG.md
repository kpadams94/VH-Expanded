# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-13

### Added

- Experienced expertise with five one-point ranks granting 10–50% more personal vault-run XP, multiplying the otherwise-earned run award. Vanilla XP and XP-granting items are unaffected.
- Repulsor specialization: an outward mob-repelling pulse and a stationary Mana Barrier, with rank and over-level scaling, native area-of-effect/mana/cooldown modifiers, and configurable tuning.
- Repulsor's original blue-ring icon, native-style descriptions, and live tuning reload.

### New Mods

- Baubley Heart Canisters integration: one two-point QOL research unlock, twelve vault-material crafting recipes, and amulet-based progression up to 40 additional full hearts.
- Research-gated Mod Box rewards for an empty amulet and miniature red, yellow, and green hearts, using the native weighted loot system.
- Ordinary miniature-heart mob drops disabled; miniature-heart consumption follows the native vault blacklist while outside-vault healing remains available.
