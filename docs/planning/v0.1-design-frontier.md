## Agreed feature direction

- Q1 answered: add **Repulsor**, a new specialization of **Mana Barrier**, combining radial mob knockback with a brief shield similar to the existing barrier.
  - Q5 answered A: one immediate outward knockback pulse on activation, followed by a brief barrier that keeps mobs out. No ongoing repulsion pulse.
  - Radius starts at 1 block at level 1 and increases by 0.5 blocks per subsequent level; applies to both repulsion and barrier.
  - Q8 answered A: duration starts at 0.5 seconds and gains 0.5 seconds at levels 4, 8, 12, 16, etc., including over-levels. Formula: 0.5 + 0.5 * floor(effective level / 4) seconds.
  - Q9 answered A: knockback aims to push affected mobs just beyond the barrier's edge, clearing its interior. Exact strength/collision handling should be tuned to that feel rather than large-distance flinging.
  - Q10 answered A: continuous barrier protection through repeated casts is an intended reward for over-levels, cooldown reduction, and sufficient mana. Do not introduce mandatory downtime to prevent that outcome. Exact recast/overlap handling remains an implementation detail to resolve consistently with this behavior.
  - Q4 answered A: cooldown starts at 20 seconds at level 1, falls by 1 second per upgrade, and reaches the 4-second floor at level 17. Formula: max(4, 21 - effective level) seconds, before cooldown reduction. This explicitly supersedes the original level-16 example.
  - User describes normal Mana Barrier progression as eight levels, with over-levels beyond eight. Research must verify the target version.
  - Cooldown reduction should apply after the base floor; 80% reduction of 4 seconds mathematically gives 0.8 seconds, subject to actual game rules.
  - Q13 answered: initial mana cost is 15 at level 1, increasing by 3 per ability level; tunable after playtesting. Working interpretation includes over-levels consistently with the other scaling: 15 + 3 * (effective level - 1).
  - Q14 answered: barrier stays at the cast location and uses Mana Barrier's configuration as baseline. Players and player abilities pass through; mobs and projectiles do not. Preserve the explicit player-ability exception; do not silently narrow projectile blocking to hostile projectiles only. Verify base behavior against these requested rules.
- Q2 answered: keep the name **Experienced**, replace its vanilla XP bonus with a vault XP bonus. Five ranks, +10% per rank, maximum +50%. Wold's implementation is a research reference, not an authorization to copy code indiscriminately.
  - Q6 answered A: boost XP earned from vault runs only. Vault-XP items are excluded.
  - Q15 answered A: multiply the otherwise-earned vault-run XP after other bonuses by 1 + 0.1 * rank. Rank 5 multiplies by 1.5; 1,000 base XP doubled by another bonus becomes 3,000 XP. Apply once; do not adopt Wold's additive stacking rule.
  - Q18 answered: each Experienced rank costs 1 expertise point, totaling 5 points at maximum rank.
- Q3 answered: integrate **Baubley Heart Canisters** into research progression. Health upgrades should be expensive and crafted with vault resources. User characterizes these as permanent upgrades; research must clarify the mod's actual equipment/consumption mechanics. Exact recipes are explicitly deferred to the separate recipe designer.
  - Q7 answered A: keep the native equipped Heart Amulet requirement. Canisters are lasting, reusable upgrades whose health benefit applies while the amulet is equipped; no innate consumable health system.
  - Q11 answered A: retain the full four-color progression, ten canisters per color and +40 full hearts in total if supported by the target release. Balance through expensive recipes. If target-release inspection contradicts these native mechanics, revisit the implementation rather than silently changing this qualified decision.
  - Q12 answered A: introduce heart-canister research in early progression. Keep the research accessible and let vault-material crafting costs control the pace of accumulating hearts. Exact knowledge costs and recipes remain deferred.
  - Q16 answered: one early research unlock covers all four heart-canister colors. Later colors do not require additional research unlocks; expensive recipes govern advancement.
  - Q18 answered with correction: heart-canister research costs **2 knowledge points**, not the proposed 1. Place it in the existing **Quality of Life (QOL)** category for v0.1.0. Exact recipes remain deferred.
