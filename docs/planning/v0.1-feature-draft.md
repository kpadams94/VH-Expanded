# Vault Hunters Expanded v0.1.0 — working feature specification

Status: historical combined draft, superseded by the individual specifications in [the spec index](../specs/README.md). Use those documents for implementation; this file preserves the design conversation's earlier consolidation.

## Purpose and target

Create three bounded additions to Vault Hunters Third Edition — Remastered, with early playable milestones that let the user judge the direction. Proposed baseline: Remastered 2.0.4, Minecraft 1.18.2, Forge, Java 17. Exact pack/build artifacts and the user's intended installation must be pinned before implementation.

Release v0.1.0 targets testing and initial play in a **fresh solo test world**. Dedicated-server and multiplayer support/validation are explicitly deferred. Existing-world migration is outside this release's validation scope. Preserve a viable path to multiplayer: keep gameplay state and awards server-authoritative within singleplayer's integrated server and keep client presentation separate. Do not claim multiplayer compatibility until it has been implemented as needed and tested in a later release.

## Experienced expertise replacement

Retain the name **Experienced**. Remove its vanilla experience bonus and replace it with a bonus to XP earned from vault runs. XP-granting items do not receive the bonus.

| Purchased rank | Vault-run XP bonus |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | 0% |
| 1 | +10% |
| 2 | +20% |
| 3 | +30% |
| 4 | +40% |
| 5 | +50% |

Percentages are cumulative rank totals, not compounding increases per rank. Apply Experienced multiplicatively to the vault-run XP that would otherwise be awarded after other bonuses: `otherwise-earned run XP * (1 + 0.1 * rank)`. Apply the expertise once. For example, a 1,000-base-XP run raised to 2,000 XP by another bonus awards 3,000 XP with rank-5 Experienced. Wold's additive stacking rule is explicitly not the desired behavior.

Each Experienced rank costs **1 expertise point**, for **5 points total** at maximum rank. Rounding remains an engineering detail to specify consistently with the target pack. Migration of existing characters' expertise investments is deferred with existing-world support; normal save/reload and rank resets in the new solo world remain in scope.

Proposed acceptance checks: each rank grants the expected run-XP bonus; vanilla XP and vault-XP items remain unaffected by this expertise; XP displays agree with the granted award; rank reset removes the benefit; a sixth purchased rank is unavailable.

Include the approved stacking example: 1,000 base XP with a separate +100% bonus and rank-5 Experienced awards 3,000 XP. Check that the benefit is applied only once and that an otherwise-zero award remains zero.

## Repulsor specialization

Add **Repulsor** as a new choice in the existing Mana Barrier/Mana Shield ability family, with exact menu placement verified against the target pack. Preserve the existing choices.

On activation, apply one outward knockback pulse to affected mobs and erect a brief barrier around the player. The intended pulse clears mobs just beyond the barrier's edge. It does not repeatedly repel for the barrier's full duration. The barrier should provide the physical mob-exclusion behavior of Mana Barrier; damage absorption has not been requested.

The barrier stays at the cast location. Use Mana Barrier's existing configuration/behavior as the integration baseline: players and player abilities pass through; mobs and projectiles are blocked. Preserve the explicit player-ability exception when an ability is implemented as a projectile. Ordinary player-fired projectiles are not automatically exempted by ownership. Verify the target Mana Barrier implementation against these requested rules before claiming equivalence.

Use eight normally purchased levels, with further progression through over-levels, subject to confirming the pack's ability machinery. Let L be the effective ability level, including over-levels:

- Raw radius: `1 + 0.5 * (L - 1)` blocks, used for both repulsion and barrier.
- Raw duration: `0.5 + 0.5 * floor(L / 4)` seconds.
- Base cooldown: `max(4, 21 - L)` seconds.
- Starting mana cost per cast: `15 + 3 * (L - 1)`. Interpret effective level consistently with the other scaling, including over-levels. These are initial playtest values and may be tuned later.
- Apply cooldown reduction after the base cooldown floor, subject to verified game rules. The floor is reached at level 17; the user's earlier level-16 example is superseded.

| Effective level | Raw radius (blocks) | Raw duration (seconds) | Base cooldown (seconds) | Mana per cast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 0.5 | 20 | 15 |
| 4 | 2.5 | 1 | 17 | 24 |
| 8 | 4.5 | 1.5 | 13 | 36 |
| 12 | 6.5 | 2 | 9 | 48 |
| 16 | 8.5 | 2.5 | 5 | 60 |
| 17 | 9 | 2.5 | 4 | 63 |
| 20 | 10.5 | 3 | 4 | 72 |

These are raw design values, not a promise about additional radius/duration gear modifiers. The implementation must resolve exact half-block barrier geometry with the existing collision system.

At level 17, 80% cooldown reduction mathematically gives 0.8 seconds before other game rules. Continuous protection through repeated casts is explicitly allowed as a reward for over-levels, cooldown reduction, and sufficient mana. Do not add mandatory downtime to prevent it. Resolve recast/overlap handling consistently with this intended outcome. Inherit remaining target-selection and collision details from verified existing Mana Barrier behavior where consistent with the approved rules above.

Proposed acceptance checks: activation produces one pulse and the brief barrier; targeted mobs clear its interior in ordinary open space; both areas follow approved radius scaling; durations increase on the specified four-level boundaries; over-levels continue scaling; base cooldown never falls below four seconds; displayed and actual values agree.

Also check the per-level mana charge, stationary placement, player/player-ability passage, and mob/projectile exclusion. Test a player ability represented internally as a projectile so the explicit ability-passage rule is preserved.

## Baubley Heart Canisters progression integration

Add the compatible Forge 1.18.2 version of **Baubley Heart Canisters** and integrate it with Vault Hunters research. Preserve its equipped Heart Amulet requirement: canisters are lasting and reusable, but their extra health applies while the amulet is equipped. No innate consumable-health replacement is in scope.

The desired acquisition direction is expensive crafting with vault materials. Exact recipes are explicitly deferred to the user's separate recipe designer. The integration should replace ordinary acquisition where necessary to maintain that intended cost; the exact disabled drops and intermediate-item recipes will be documented after target-artifact inspection.

Retain all four colors, ten canisters per color, and a total ceiling of +40 full hearts if confirmed in the target release. The user explicitly chose to balance this full progression through expensive recipes. If target-release inspection contradicts these native mechanics, revisit the implementation rather than silently changing the qualified decision.

Make research accessible in early progression at a cost of **2 knowledge points**, placed in the existing **Quality of Life (QOL)** category for v0.1.0. Vault-material crafting costs should control the pace of accumulating hearts, rather than delaying access to the entire system until midgame or late game. Verify the target pack's category configuration and any cost modifiers when implementing this requested cost; research enforcement behavior remains to settle. Recipes remain deferred to the recipe designer.

Use one research unlock covering all four heart-canister colors. Later colors require no additional research unlocks; their recipes govern advancement.

Proposed acceptance checks: research and recipes appear correctly; agreed restrictions govern acquisition/use; equipping and removing the amulet applies and removes the expected bonus; health stacks correctly with Vault Hunters progression and survives normal save/reload transitions; ordinary mob drops cannot bypass the agreed crafting progression.

## Proposed delivery approach

Future direction, outside v0.1.0: the user is considering a separate research category tentatively named **Enhancement** for additional mods that substantially increase player power. The name and future mod roster are not settled. Keep heart canisters in QOL for this release; do not create the future category yet.

Start with Experienced as the smallest playable change; test Repulsor's pulse-and-barrier feel before expanding its full scaling; complete heart-canister progression when recipe decisions are available. This order is a recommendation, not an approved schedule.

Prefer existing configuration and recipe tooling for research, drops, and recipes. Use a Java addon for new behavior and narrowly scoped mixins where verified extension points cannot satisfy the feature. Exact engineering choices belong in the final handoff after source inspection; they do not require the user to choose a file layout.
