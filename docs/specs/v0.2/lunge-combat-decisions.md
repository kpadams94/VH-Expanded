# Lunge combat decisions — 2026-09-14

User-approved design answers for #23 through LU-22 (movement feel explicitly deferred to playtesting), with numerical follow-up. This records design acceptance, not gameplay tests. Movement starting values and no target cap are approved; movement feel awaits the explicitly deferred in-game playtest; production implementation belongs to #25.

## Targets and motion

- **LU-7:** Any normally targetable enemy is eligible, including passive/neutral animals and non-party players where normally targetable. Do not restrict targeting to hostile mobs. Party players and tamed animals receive no Lunge damage, including the Impact burst. Ignore them in Lunge's impact detection, retaining normal physical entity collision. Preserve normal targetability/invulnerability rules.
- **LU-8, revised by LU-22:** Sample aim once per cast and use Dash's native directional transform, including its upward tilt and pitch coefficient. Add the impulse to existing velocity; preserve normal movement inputs, ground/midair/water use and native elytra physics. Do not continually retarget the impulse with camera movement.
- **LU-9:** While an elytra is active, apply the same aimed kinetic influence without taking over, disabling, or artificially starting gliding. Native elytra steering/physics remain active. Enemy contact still ends the lunge and resets all momentum, including falling/gliding momentum. This is a one-time stop; do not freeze the player afterward.
- Retain ordinary fall damage. Enemy impact must also clear accumulated fall distance so lunging into an enemy while falling can arrest the fall as requested. Future falling after impact accumulates normally. Merely casting, timing out, or hitting terrain does not grant a Lunge fall-distance reset or immunity. This fall-distance handling implements the approved outcome; zero velocity alone is insufficient in Minecraft.

## Branches and damage

- **LU-12:** Approved names: **Lunge** (heavy primary), **Flurry** (rapid-fire), **Impact** (area), **Siphon** (leech).
- **LU-10, revised by LU-16:** Impact bursts only on contact with an eligible target or terrain. Natural expiry, recasting, and cancellation without contact produce no burst. This supersedes the earlier expiry-burst rule. Enemy contact deals the main strike; burst damage is a scaling percentage of configured flat-plus-AD strike damage, independent of the enemy struck, including terrain impacts. Walls block the burst. Burst percentage, radius and Nova falloff are selected below; there is no numerical target cap. Both Impact's AD coefficient and its burst percentage grow with levels, with slower strike scaling than primary Lunge.
- **LU-16:** Allow another cast before the current dive ends whenever ordinary cooldown and mana requirements permit. Apply a fresh impulse along the newly sampled aim while preserving existing momentum/native movement. Recasting does not synthesize an impact or queue extra bursts: replace/refresh the active dive tracking with the new cast, with one impact payload on its next eligible contact. This supports chained movement; it does not create multiple deferred explosions at one collision. Expiry, death, dimension change, ability removal, or other cancellation without contact ends tracking without a burst.
- The user delegated whether the main target also receives the burst. Selected here: **include the main target**, so it receives main damage plus its burst share. Other eligible targets receive only the burst. Prefer one combined damage application to the main target, avoiding a second immediate hit being rejected by native hurt immunity. This is an engineering selection within the user's delegated choice; the burst multiplier is set by LU-20.
- **LU-11:** Siphon follows native Vault leech restoration semantics, including native boss exceptions and overkill handling, while preserving normal healing modifiers/restrictions. For ordinary targets, before healing modifiers: `playerMaxHealth × min(leechFraction, hitDamage / targetMaxHealth)`. Native `VaultBoss`/`TheVesselEntity` exceptions bypass the damage-relative limit. Do not introduce a remaining-target-health clamp absent from native semantics. LU-21 selects 25% plus five percentage points every two rank-ups, capped at 80% from rank 23.
- Preserve the already-approved dedicated initial-strike healing integration: no unintended weapon procs, stale melee-charge gate, or duplicate automatic leech. Matching native health restoration does not authorize enabling specialized weapon procs. Successful damage, mitigation and healing prevention need runtime checks.
- **LU-13:** Preserve ordinary hurt immunity for Flurry and other Lunge hits; no repeat-hit bypass. Flurry is intended for moving between targets. The user accepts same-target equal hits being rejected at extreme cooldown reduction, including the 0.4-second cadence at 80% CDR. Do not add a new cooldown floor to hide this behavior.
- Primary Lunge alone retains normal direct weapon effects with their native chances/counts and modifiers. The three specializations retain their existing exclusion of direct weapon procs.

## Damage and tier follow-up

LU-14 accepts player attack damage for all four branches, evaluated on impact and independent of melee charge, with the flat-plus-AD formulas in the balance table. Do not add a full ordinary melee hit, AP scaling, or ordinary melee crit/sweep bonuses. Preserve primary native proc eligibility/chances/counts through deliberate ability context.

LU-15 accepts eight learnable ranks and 30 defined effective tiers, holding configured values at tier 30 above that extent. Each learned rank costs one point; no level/prerequisite gate. Damage continues scaling through all defined over-levels, as does Siphon's native leech fraction (25% plus five percentage points every two rank-ups, capped at 80%; no separate global healing-effectiveness bonus). See [balance table](lunge-balance.md) for the latest cooldown directions, which supersede the original Flurry tier-eight two-second target.

- **LU-17:** Flurry starts at 12 seconds at rank one, subtracts one second per rank, and stops at two seconds: `max(2, 12-(L-1))`. Rank eight is five seconds; rank ten is three; rank eleven and above are two. This supersedes earlier rank-eight/rank-ten endpoints. Retain ordinary CDR and native hurt immunity; the two-second cap is a base cooldown, not a final cooldown floor.

LU-18 sets Flurry, Impact and Siphon to 3 flat damage at rank one, increasing by 1 per rank through tier 30. Their AD coefficient remains 10% initially plus 5 percentage points per rank.

### Accepted numerical rules — LU-19–LU-22 follow-up

- At effective tier L (1–30), Lunge damage is `5 + 2*(L-1) + (10% + 10%*(L-1))*AD`; Flurry/Impact/Siphon damage is `3 + (L-1) + (10% + 5%*(L-1))*AD`. Eight learnable ranks, one point each, no level/prerequisite gate; 30 defined tiers, holding final values above 30. Impact-time AD, independent of melee charge; no added ordinary melee hit, AP component or ordinary melee crit/sweep bonus.
- Base cooldowns: Lunge `20 - 0.5*(min(L,12)-1)` seconds; Impact/Siphon `30 - 0.5*(min(L,12)-1)` seconds; Flurry `max(2,13-L)` seconds. Flurry reaches its two-second base cap at rank eleven; native CDR/refunds and hurt immunity remain.
- LU-19 mana: Lunge `15 + 3*(L-1)`; Flurry `12 + (L-1)`; Impact and Siphon 25 at every tier. Flurry rank-eight mana is 19, superseding the earlier 20–25 target. Curves extend through tier 30: Lunge 102, Flurry 41, Impact/Siphon 25.
- LU-20 Impact burst: `(30% + 5 percentage points*(L-1))` of configured Impact strike damage; may exceed 100%, reaching 175% at tier 30. Fixed base radius 2 blocks, scaled by applicable native percentage area modifiers/limits. Adopt Nova's five distance bands: 100/80/60/40/20% damage over successive fifths of the effective radius, measured from collision origin. No numerical target cap; affect all eligible targets in range, as with Nova. Walls block the burst; main target receives main hit plus its burst share. Only target/terrain contact triggers the burst.
- LU-21 Siphon leech fraction: `min(80%, 25% + 5 percentage points*floor((L-1)/2))`. Ranks 1–2:25%, 3–4:30%, reaching 80% at rank 23 and remaining there. Use native boss/overkill and healing restrictions. This scales Siphon's own fraction, not a global healing-effectiveness stat. Damage continues increasing after the leech cap.
- LU-22 approved starting movement: 90% of Bullet's impulse curve at every effective tier: `0.9 * (10 + 1 + floor(L/2)) * 0.15`, before the native directional coefficient. Use Dash's ten-degree upward adjustment, vertical clamp and pitch-dependent strength, adding velocity to existing momentum. This explicitly supersedes exact-aim/no-upward-offset requirements. Sample direction once per cast; ordinary movement input and native elytra physics remain. No midair timer: end on landing or target/terrain impact, with lifecycle cleanup. Chained casts remain allowed. Exact feel is explicitly deferred to the first in-game playtest; tweak if enemies/terrain make it awkward. Preserve compact-body clearance and first-enemy stop as required behavior, and do not import Dash-only etchings.
- These are selected design values, not runtime acceptance. All player-facing choices are answered. Movement feel is explicitly deferred to in-game tuning. Heart fragmentation is a native on-kill effect: the pinned PlayerEvents death listener checks the native Lucky Hit flag guard (including AOE/chaining), Vault location, player damage-source owner, positive HIT_HEARTS and its native random chance. Primary Lunge may generate it through an eligible lethal strike at native chance; specialization damage must retain the suppressing flags. This does not remove ordinary player kill credit or kill-cooldown talent events. Runtime checks remain required.

## Required implementation checks

Test target filtering (animals, tamed animals, party/non-party players); sampled aim with ordinary input in air/water and while gliding; first swept contact; overhead clearance; momentum and fall-distance reset on enemy contact; ordinary fall damage on misses/terrain/expiry and after a subsequent fall; one Impact burst on eligible target/terrain contact and none on expiry/recast/cancellation; full primary-plus-burst damage without a second immunity check; native Flurry immunity across same and different targets; native Siphon boss/overkill/healing restrictions; proc isolation; restoration and client/server agreement. Test chained casts before dive completion, refreshed collision tracking without duplicate payloads, and cleanup without bursts on death, dimension change, ability removal, or expiry.
